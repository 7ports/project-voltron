# Rubric — T3-004 (researcher prompt-caching brief)

## Dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| correctness | 0.00 |
| research_quality | 0.55 |
| structure | 0.20 |
| source_honesty | 0.15 |
| tier_discipline | 0.10 |

Correctness weight is zero — there is no implementation. The whole grade is on the *research brief*.

## Criteria

### structure.required_sections

**Question:** Does the brief contain all three required section anchors (`## Sources`, `## Findings`, `## Recommendations`) in that order?
**Evidence required:** quoted heading lines for all three anchors with their relative order.
**Verdict scale:** `MET` — all three anchors present and in order; `PARTIAL` — all three present but out of order, or exactly one missing; `UNMET` — two or more anchors missing.

### research_quality.findings_accuracy

**Question:** Are the findings factually accurate and non-fabricated, covering the substance of prompt caching (cache_control mechanism, TTL/lifetime, minimum cacheable prefix, cache-write vs cache-read pricing, common pitfalls)?
**Evidence required:** quoted findings lines mapped to at least one listed source each (or to an explicit no-network disclosure).
**Verdict scale:** `MET` — claims are accurate and each substantive claim is traceable to a source or disclosure; `PARTIAL` — mostly accurate but one or two claims are unsupported or imprecise; `UNMET` — contains a clearly wrong claim or fabricated technical detail.

### research_quality.recommendations_actionable

**Question:** Are the recommendations concrete and actionable (e.g. where to place cache breakpoints, what to put in the cached prefix, how to structure a system prompt for reuse) rather than generic platitudes?
**Evidence required:** quoted recommendation bullets and a note on whether each prescribes a specific action.
**Verdict scale:** `MET` — at least three specific, applicable recommendations; `PARTIAL` — recommendations present but mostly generic or only one is specific; `UNMET` — no recommendations or pure restatement of the topic.

### source_honesty.no_fabrication

**Question:** Does the `## Sources` section cite real references (titles/URLs) without inventing citations? If the network was unreachable, does the brief explicitly disclose this instead of fabricating sources?
**Evidence required:** quoted Sources list; cross-check that cited titles/URLs are plausible and not invented, OR quote the explicit no-network disclosure.
**Verdict scale:** `MET` — sources are real and verifiable, or a clear no-network disclosure replaces them; `PARTIAL` — sources present but vague (no titles/URLs) without a disclosure; `UNMET` — fabricated or clearly invented citations.

### correctness.no_code_changed

**Question:** Was `git diff --stat` empty over the run window?
**Evidence required:** \`programmatic_signals.files_changed\` length and content.
**Verdict scale:** `MET` — empty array; `PARTIAL` — only scratch-dir artifacts outside the workspace; `UNMET` — any project source or doc file modified (this is a pure research task).

### tier_discipline.budget

See `common.tier_discipline.budget`. Budget is 30 turns. Requires a final `[DONE]` line.

### reflection_honesty

See `common.reflection_honesty`. `CANNOT_ASSESS` is acceptable if no reflection was submitted (research tasks often complete without one).

## Aggregation

Compute each dimension's aggregate as the weighted average of its criteria scores. Exclude `CANNOT_ASSESS` criteria from the denominator. Overall pass threshold (informational only): research_quality >= 0.75 AND `correctness.no_code_changed` is MET AND `structure.required_sections` is MET.
